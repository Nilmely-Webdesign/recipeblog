Tomato Soup

A simple 5 ingredient tomato soup made with butter, onion, and canned San Marzano tomatoes. Can be eaten with garlic bread,
grilled cheese, and so much more.
Overview
  • Time: 50 minutes
  • Serving size: 4-5
  • Calories: 284
  • Can be dairy-free if dairy-free butter
    is used

Serving soup with garlic bread

 Ingredients
  • Two 28-ounce cans San
    Marzano
  • 1 stick (8 tbsp) salted butter
  • 1 yellow onion, peel removed,
    cut into 4 chunks
  • 3-4 cloves smashed garlic
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt

Pouring tomato paste into pot

 Steps
  • Put the tomatoes, butter, and
    onion in a large saucepan or
    Dutch oven. Bring it up to a
    low bubble,
    then turn the heat to medium low.
    Cover partially with a lid; let it
    simmer for 45 minutes. Stir every
    15 minutes or so to prevent
    scorching on
    the bottom.
  • Remove the onion pieces
    using tongs
  • Using an immersion blender,
    blend up the soup until
    it’s a smooth as you want it.
  • Taste and adjust; you can thin
    it out with milk, water,
    broth, cream, whatever you want
  • Serve tomato soup with garlic bread,
    grilled cheese, croutons, crackers,
    pesto, whatever you like!
